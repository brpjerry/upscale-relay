This release makes the desktop client's interface move at the display's
refresh rate during playback, makes resizing the video smooth, and removes
most of the judder in fullscreen playback.

- The interface no longer steps at the video's frame rate. mpv now hands
  each frame over at its display time instead of about one frame early, so
  its render call no longer holds the interface thread for most of every
  frame. Sidebar slides went from 12-15 to about 120 fps, fullscreen
  transitions from 24-29 to 107-117 fps, and the fullscreen control bar from
  about 15 to about 110 position updates a second (measured on a 120 Hz
  laptop panel on AC power; about 80-100 fps on battery). Video timing
  improved with it: 99.6% of 24 fps frames land on their refresh, against
  85% before.
- The sidebar resizes live while its edge is dragged, about 2 px and 15 ms
  behind the pointer, instead of showing a rubber band.
- Entering and leaving fullscreen no longer makes the picture jump to its
  final size. The video follows the sliding chrome step by step, drawn
  scaled from its last render while it moves and sharp again once it
  settles; the control bar slides into its fullscreen place instead of
  leaving the layout at once.
- Fullscreen playback judders far less. The automatic accent colour now
  samples the picture only when a video opens, after a seek and on pause:
  its screenshot every 2 s put 13-22% of frames a refresh or more off
  schedule in fullscreen playback from the server, against about 1% now.
- The Video timing switch is renamed "Sync video to the display while
  windowed" and says what it does: it lets mpv's display synchronization
  modes and motion interpolation take effect. It still pauses in fullscreen,
  where display pacing costs about 3 W on battery and a demanding mpv.conf
  could not render every refresh. The hints no longer claim that the
  interface updates once per video frame, and they say when motion
  interpolation is set but cannot take effect.

The Arch Linux client package and Windows server ZIPs use version 0.23.0.
No protocol version change is required.
